FUTURE_PE_03 

AI SEO Content Generator for Coaching Institutes

Business Chosen

Bright Future Academy – A coaching institute in Rajahmundry offering EAMCET, IIT-JEE, and NEET preparation for students.

---

Target Audience

- Students (Class 10–12)
- Parents searching for coaching institutes
- Competitive exam aspirants

---

Project Objective

To design a structured prompt engineering system that generates SEO-optimized blogs and content clusters for coaching institutes.

This system produces:

- Pillar blogs (long-form SEO articles)
- Supporting blogs (content clusters)
- SEO-friendly headings (H1–H3)
- Internal linking strategies
- Local SEO content

---

Problem Identified

Many coaching institutes:

- Do not rank on Google
- Publish unstructured blogs
- Miss local SEO opportunities
- Lack keyword-focused content
- Fail to convert visitors into leads

---

Prompt Engineering Approach

Keyword + Intent Targeting

Prompts focus on:

- Informational intent
- Local search intent
- Decision-making intent

Content Cluster Strategy

- 1 Pillar Blog
- 3–5 Supporting Blogs
- Internal linking between them

SEO Structure Control

- H1, H2, H3 headings
- Readable paragraphs
- Keyword placement

Local SEO Optimization

- City-based keywords
- Coaching + location targeting
